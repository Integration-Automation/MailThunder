## U-20261001-01 · 2026-10-01 · Every workflow job has a timeout · #ci #tests

- **What**: every job that runs on a runner sets `timeout-minutes`, about three times its slowest recent run and at least 15 minutes. Without it a hung job runs for GitHub's default 360 minutes and holds the runner. Timeouts: `publish_stable.yml`: `test` 30, `publish` 15; `test_dev.yml`: `test` 30; `test_stable.yml`: `test` 30.
- **Tests**: `test/unit_test/test_workflow_actions.py` `test_every_job_has_a_timeout` fails for any job with `runs-on` and no `timeout-minutes`; the previous workflows fail it.
- **Files**: `.github/workflows/publish_stable.yml`, `.github/workflows/test_dev.yml`, `.github/workflows/test_stable.yml`, `test/unit_test/test_workflow_actions.py`.

## U-20261001-02 · 2026-10-01 · Executor and its helpers move to je_action_core · #migration #executor #L-6
